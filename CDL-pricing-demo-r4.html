Questions about quoting

Why is there no price list?
Because coverage is scoped to your legal entities, and no two importers have the same set. You are only ever covered for, and billed for, the entities you actually have. Most quotes are turned around the same day.
What counts as a legal entity?
Every legal name your shipments arrive under. Name variations, including misspellings, are not separate entities and are always covered.
Can we start with one entity and add more later?
Yes. Adding an entity moves you to the plan that fits, and renewal dates line up so nothing lapses in between.
Do we have to change brokers or forwarders?
No. Nothing changes about how you ship. We handle the protection and the upkeep.
