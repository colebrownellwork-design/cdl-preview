Questions about quoting

Why is there no price list?
Because coverage is scoped to your legal entities, and no two importers have the same set. You are only ever covered for, and billed for, the entities you actually have. Most quotes come back within a week.
What counts as a legal entity?
A legal entity is a registered company that imports under its own name. A name variation is that same company spelled differently on a shipment, by a forwarder or a supplier. We scope and quote by entity. Variations are never counted or capped: every one you give us is covered, and every new one we find is added.
Can we start with one entity and add more later?
Yes. Adding an entity moves you to the plan that fits, and renewal dates line up so nothing lapses in between.
Do we have to change brokers or forwarders?
No. Nothing changes about how you ship. We handle the protection and the upkeep.
